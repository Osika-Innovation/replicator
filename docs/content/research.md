## Materials as tones

The materials research programme explores the relationship between structure and response across scales. Atomic transition frequencies, lattice vibrations and component resonances each provide useful information. The [materials guide](materials.html) connects those descriptions to fabrication and inspection.

The broader periodic-table research proposes an underlying standing-wave interpretation of the elements. That is a research model, distinct from the measured spectra and established crystal structure used in component design. Its private source paper is not copied into this public site.

## Source reports and design questions

Reports of highly ordered or unusual materials can motivate a search for simple geometry and useful material structure. Turn such a lead into an attributable source, a specific composition or microstructure, a measurement and a manufacturing question.

Confidence in the programme belongs in its objectives and execution. Claims about the composition or origin of a reported artifact require their own evidence; they do not set a fabrication specification by repetition.

## How a source becomes engineering

Record the DOI, patent number or versioned source; distinguish a screenshot lead, abstract and full-method review. Identify the mechanism, task, required precision, material/process dependencies and next discriminating check.

Retain negative findings and unresolved leads with reasons. Promote a source into a module only through a concrete design and acceptance test.

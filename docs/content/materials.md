# Materials, modes and crystal engineering

We design the Replicator's shape and its material structure together. The aim is more function in fewer parts: a body that guides vibration, a surface that shapes a field, a crystal that links electrical and mechanical motion, or an optical element that does one repeatable job on light. Every candidate earns its place by its response, manufacturing route and measured benefit (Design decision).

This is the fabrication end of the [physics section](physics.html). [Periodic table and tones](periodic-table.html) maps material response. [Light, fields and matter](light-matter.html) gives the interpretation.

## Spectra across scales

“Materials as tones” is useful if the scale and the thing measured are stated. Atoms have electronic transitions with characteristic frequencies. Crystals carry collective vibrations of their lattice, the regular grid of atoms. A finished component has resonances set by its material, shape, mounting and conditions. These related descriptions use different equations and measurement methods (Documented, textbook physics). Each material record therefore keeps spectra, mechanical and electrical properties, composition, processing history and uncertainty. [Atomic spectra and energy levels](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-spectra-database)

## Quartz and the cymatics question

In quartz, silicon and oxygen atoms form an ordered network of linked four-cornered units (tetrahedra). The structure has a handedness, and its symmetry permits [piezoelectric](glossary.html) coupling: squeezing the crystal produces electrical polarization, and an electric field produces strain (Documented). How a quartz component couples to a drive depends on its crystal cut, dimensions, electrodes and mounting. A measured resonance is a property of that complete system. [Quartz structure and applications](https://www.iycr2014.org/__data/assets/pdf_file/0008/89288/50-K-UK09-Structure-et-applications-du-quartz-Sup.pdf), [experimental quartz characterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00284)

Cymatics is the study of patterns that vibration makes in sand or liquid. It suggests a question: can chosen fields guide matter toward a useful ordered state (Open)? A quartz lattice is held together by atomic bonds, and its vibration modes describe motion around that structure. Growing quartz needs the right composition and a process that controls how the first tiny crystals start, how they grow, and where flaws appear. Combining the separate spectral lines of silicon and oxygen is not an established recipe for it. The task is to find a drive that changes one formation mechanism, and to show the resulting structural improvement.

## From arrangement to a permanent structure

Acoustic assembly is the starting point. Published experiments have arranged microparticles into three-dimensional structures using several acoustic holograms, then fixed them by gelation (Documented). This shows a route from designed fields to lasting particle structures, within the reported materials and conditions. It also names four jobs: transport the feedstock, form the arrangement, consolidate it and inspect the result ([Manufacturing](manufacturing.html#the-loop-feed-shape-lock-check)). [Holographic assembly in three dimensions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9908023/)

## Optical-wavelength structures

[Metasurfaces](glossary.html), surfaces patterned more finely than the wavelength, are a core lane of material design. The [optics guide](optics.html#fabricating-the-next-optical-module) connects what they do to light with feature sizes, optical constants, master and replication processes, and inspection. The lane advances alongside the acoustic prototype. The first optics or masters are made outside and seed later machine-made upgrades.

## The materials upgrade path

The sequence is a Design decision. First, qualify field-shaped assemblies and replaceable acoustic structures. Next, introduce controlled texture, orientation, porosity or composition where they improve a named component. Pursue optical-grade or single-crystal modules once their feature size, surface quality and defect limits have a credible fabrication and inspection route.

The [twin](digital-twin.html) connects four records for every upgrade: the desired material state, the field and process programme, the fabrication capability, and the inspection evidence. We optimize performance per part and human effort per verified improvement. Simpler shape is valuable when material and process carry the function reliably. [The upgrade loop](upgrades.html) records each step.

## Why three cavities: the 8πV / 3λ³ mode-count theorem

A natural early temptation was to use the Replicator's acoustic build volume directly as the computer. Mode counting rules that out immediately (`Derived`). For a cavity of volume `V` operating at wavelength `λ`, the number of independent electromagnetic or acoustic modes scales as:

    N ≈ 8π V / (3 λ³)

Evaluating this bound in an `18.5 L` reference chamber shows why compute, memory, and actuation must occupy separate physical wavelengths:

| Carrier in an `18.5 L` volume | Wavelength `λ` | Independent modes `N` | Engineering role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Acoustic (`100 kHz` in air) | `3.4 mm` | `3.8 × 10⁶` | **Build chamber (actuator):** enough modes to sculpt grains, too few for computation |
| Acoustic (`~1 MHz` in melt) | `~5 mm` | `1.2 × 10⁶` | **Coupled work fluid:** higher trapping power density at millimeter wavelengths |
| Microwave (`10 GHz`) | `30 mm` | `5.7 × 10³` | **Positioning & heating aid:** fewer spatial modes than `100 kHz` sound; earns its place through speed and dielectric selectivity, not mode capacity |
| Optical (`500 nm`) | `0.5 µm` | `1.2 × 10¹⁸` | **Compute core & holographic store:** twelve orders of magnitude denser information capacity |

A `10 GHz` microwave field actually has *fewer* spatial modes in the same box than `100 kHz` ultrasound because `λ` is larger in air (`30 mm` vs. `3.4 mm`). The architecture therefore assigns **one mathematical operation**—recording and re-illuminating interference `Z(r) = X₀ + M · Re{Ψ*_ref · Ψ_obj}`—across three physical wavelengths rather than forcing force-grade sound waves to perform neural arithmetic.

## Holographic memory: two layers and chords, not bits

An earlier draft split memory across four distinct tiers (fast external modulator, active medium, PCM cache, write-once archive). Both outer tiers collapse: an acoustic build frame changes on milliseconds, whereas an optical cavity settles in nanoseconds, and non-volatile phase-change materials retain state without power. Two physical layers suffice:

| Layer | Role | Physical substrate | Persistence & timescale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Live working state** | Propagating equilibrium state during inference, recall, or control | `Cadence` complex SRAM/BRAM in **Prototype**; optical / excitable wave cavity in **Ultimate** | One coherence or settle window (`ps–ns` optical, `µs–ms` prototype digital) |
| **Non-volatile store** | Persistent library of object chords, scattering priors, and calibration transforms | Compact `.pattern` / `AssociativeChordBank` on storage in **Prototype**; low-loss `Sb₂S₃` / `Sb₂Se₃` PCM or photorefractive crystals in **Ultimate** | Zero standby power; high write endurance reserved for slow learning updates |

### Volumetric mode accounting vs. the `(M# / M)²` noise wall (`Derived`)

Treating a thick hologram as a 2D surface bit grid (`~200 MB/cm²`) understates volumetric Bragg selectivity. Counting `(λ/2)³` Nyquist cells across a `1 cm³` volume with 4 bits per mode (amplitude, phase, polarization) gives a geometric mode ceiling around `~32 TB/cm³`—enough to fit a `1024 × 1024` complex matrix inside a cube roughly `51 µm` on a side.

Geometric mode counts, however, are **never** the bottleneck in a real system. Multiplexing `M` pages inside the same volume splits a finite refractive-index budget `M#`, causing recall diffraction efficiency per page to drop quadratically:

    η = (M# / M)²

At a realistic `M# = 20`: storing `100` pages yields `η ≈ 4 × 10⁻²`; `1,000` pages drops to `4 × 10⁻⁴`; and `10,000` pages drops to `4 × 10⁻⁶`. Long before reaching the `λ³` geometric limit, detector shot noise and crosstalk stop usable recall. For this reason:

* **Low-loss photonic chalcogenides (`Sb₂S₃`, `Sb₂Se₃`)** replace GST (`Ge₂Sb₂Te₅`), because crystalline GST absorbs strongly where phase-only modulation (`Δn ~ 1`) is needed to raise `M#`. Fabricating thick, low-loss volumetric multi-layer PCM stacks remains `Open`.
* **Chords, not raw bits:** The native object file (`.pattern`) already represents complex boundary poles and port vectors. Storing interferometric chords directly deletes an unnecessary bit-serialization layer and enables parallel matched-filter correlation ("what looks like this boundary echo?").
* **Strict provenance firewall:** Associative recall—whether from an optical correlator or `Cadence`'s Modern-Hopfield chord bank—returns ranked hypotheses, never direct ground truth. Every `.pattern` chord must explicitly tag `"measured"` vs. `"inferred"` with mandatory `"inferredBy"` attribution and zero default value ([Matter loop](matter-loop.html#scan-non-moving-3d-tomography-and-scattering); compiler gate `G17`).

## The Cadence wave-equilibrium model (EqProp)

Whether executed in software during prototype training or implemented physically in a wave medium, the Replicator's internal model (`Cadence`) avoids fragile deep backpropagation graphs and models chambers natively in the wave domain (`Simulated` in `holo/`).

### 1. Complex Hermitian graph and Equilibrium Propagation (`EqProp`)

Nodes in `Cadence` represent complex phasor wave-somas `z = a exp(iφ) ∈ ℂ` (carrying both amplitude and wave phase). Forward and feedback connections obey tied **Helmholtz Hermitian symmetry** inside a contractive `1`-Lipschitz spectral bound:

    W_fb = W_fwdᴴ        ||W||₂ ≤ 1   (typically ≤ 0.68 in Vision-Motor Gate H-13)

Contractive relaxation acts as a physical error-correcting fixed point: transient noise decays across iterations instead of amplifying down a feedforward chain. Learning uses centered **Equilibrium Propagation (`EqProp`)** ([Scellier & Bengio, 2017](https://doi.org/10.3389/fncom.2017.00024)): clamp or weakly nudge outputs with `+β` and `−β` around the free equilibrium, let the wavefield settle to fixed points `z⁺` and `z⁻`, and update weights strictly locally:

    ΔW_ij = η (z_i⁺ conj(z_j⁺) − z_i⁻ conj(z_j⁻))

Notice why this maps directly onto future physical wave hardware: optical reciprocity in lossless/symmetric media (`ε = εᵀ`) supplies the conjugate-transpose feedback `Wᴴ` automatically when light propagates in reverse, and two-beam optical interference between the free and nudged fields computes the local outer product directly inside a rewritable medium (`Derived`; `Open` hardware test).

### 2. Intra-settle physics alignment (`holo/`)

To keep neural settling consistent with real acoustic and optical chamber physics rather than treating the solver as an opaque black box:

* **Barrier-free chaotic relaxation:** Custom GPU Metal kernels run asynchronous Chazan–Miranker Gauss–Seidel relaxation in shared threadgroup memory so nodes settle without global clock barriers.
* **Three nested timescales (`dt_out > dt_upper > dt_core`):** Fast chamber boundary nodes (`v_o`), intermediate upper geometry layers (`v_u`), and slower latent attractors (`v_core`) relax together without oscillation.
* **Symmetric port-impedance self-coupling:** Output neurons carry an explicit chamber-impedance term `W_oo = −λ (ΨΦ)ᴴ (ΨΦ)`, ensuring neural equilibrium and acoustic port admittance share a single Lyapunov energy surface.
* **Live associative chord bank (`AssociativeChordBank`):** During live settling, `v_o` couples to a complex Modern-Hopfield bank of compact `346 B` `FCD1` / `.pattern` chords so high-precision 3D shapes recall in one settle without overwriting existing spatial priors.
* **Zero-retraining body swap (`ThoracicMotorCompiler`):** Peripheral microcontrollers demodulate thermal `c₀(T)` drift (analogous to insect Johnston's organs) and subtract local `160 dB` efference leaks before transmitting boundary innovations (`343` complex axons on a `7×7×7` nerve). The central `Cadence` core solves geometry in canonical space and projects through each machine's calibrated `body-response/0.1` transfer matrix `H(f)` into `.fcode` (`ADD`, `REMOVE = conj(drives)`, `LISTEN = 10⁻³ × drives`), transferring across distinct plate geometries (`borosilicate_12port`, `ha0_boro12`, `vogel_plates`, `icosa_12port`) with zero retraining.

## 3D form-drawing curriculum, OPH quarantine and teacher distillation

In simulation (`holo/`), `Cadence` is trained and evaluated under strict epistemic isolation before touching hardware (`Simulated`):

* **Core-13 + Upgrade-STL Curriculum:** Five Platonic polyhedra (`T_d` tetrahedron, `O_h` cube/octahedron, `I_h` dodecahedron/icosahedron) and eight Grade-1/2 constructor primitives (`sphere`, `ring`, `rod`, `bowl`, `triad`, `helix`, `horn`, `waveguide`), plus real prototype `P0` and `HA-0` upgrade STL parts (`p0_aperture_plate`, `ha0_sla_phase_ring`, `ha0_bayonet_ring`, `ha0_septum_cap`, `ha0_front_clamp`), sampled across continuous `SO(3)` orientations in `6`-stroke `liquid_gorkov` (mass-conserved fluid morphing) and `static_exposure` regimes.
* **Strict 2D OPH boundary quarantine:** 3D ground-truth voxel volumes are used solely in training losses and held-out IoU verification. During operation, `Cadence` sees **only** physical boundary observables (`HAPTIC` acoustic `S(f)` ports + Beer–Lambert / `LightRead` `SMI` rays along true `HA-0` CAD sightlines, strictly zero rays through opaque walls), tested under full multimodal operation as well as `acoustic_blind` (`optical = 0`) and `optical_smi_only` (`acoustic = 0`) ablations.
* **Multi-teacher world & 3D distillation:** Open-weight foundation models (`TRELLIS` 3D Gaussians, `DINOv2` multi-view vision, `V-JEPA2` spatiotemporal dynamics predictor) are offline-distilled on the workstation into compact `96-d` chord descriptors grafted via SVD spectral bridges—keeping giant transformers off the live machine.
* **Physical solver & hardware-realism gates:** All `Cadence` proposals are compared directly against the twin's physics optimizer (`NOWS`) and stressed across four hardware tiers (`ideal_fp32`, `rp2350_ganglia_q8` with `1-bit ΣΔ` quantization and `C₀(T)` drift, `fpga_int8_sparse`, and `optical_pcm_analog`).

## Compute substrate ladder: from prototype SRAM to near-zero-electricity wave media

Notice the central architectural rule: **the mathematical equations (`W_fb = Wᴴ`, `||W||₂ < 1`, `+β/−β EqProp`, sparse `.pattern` chords) remain invariant across every substrate rung, while electrical power and discrete silicon drop away.**

| Rung | Role & status | Compute mechanism | Storage & speed | Electrical energy & honest limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Prototype Host Trainer** | Software research & offline curriculum trainer (`Simulated`) | Apple Silicon unified-memory `MLX` + custom Metal chaotic relaxation kernels | Full `fp32` training across `SO(3)` batches | Workstation wall power (`~10–100 W`); excluded from standalone appliance loop |
| **2 · Prototype Embedded Brain Slot (`P0` / `HA-0`)** | Low-cost workshop bootstrap controller (`Design decision` / `Derived`; coupons open) | Card 1: `5–10%` block-sparse, event-driven `int8` `Cadence` on `RP2350B` SRAM (`< 60 kB` vs. `372 kB` dense); Card 2: optional `SoC-FPGA` dual-port BRAM | `~8–15 ms` settle on `RP2350B` (vs. `~220–300 ms` dense); `< 5 µs` on `SoC-FPGA` | Milliwatts to low watts; deliberate prototype scaffolding, not the long-term machine identity |
| **3 · Optical Cavity + PCM Core** | First physical wave-compute Brain Slot (`Published` component physics; composed loop `Open`) | Threshold laser diodes natively implement `eml(x, y) = exp(x) − ln(y)`; passive `Sb₂S₃`/`Sb₂Se₃` hologram executes `W` & reciprocal `Wᴴ` at the speed of light | Sub-nanosecond propagation per layer; non-volatile chord storage (`η = (M#/M)²`) | Millijoule-to-sub-picojoule per op potential; bounded today by resin cavity `Q_eff ≈ 10` (vs. CVD diamond `Q > 10⁹`), `4–8` analog bits, and `10⁶–10⁹` PCM write cycles |
| **4 · Ultimate Minimal-Electricity Wave Substrates** | Long-term optimal Replicator compute fabric (`Research hypothesis` / `Open`) | Passive optical/phononic holographic networks paired with excitable soft-matter / neural-gel wave substrates whose intrinsic chemical, dielectric, or mechanical relaxation directly settles `EqProp` fixed points | Continuous analog spatial state with self-maintaining memory and local plasticity | Aims at minimal-electricity operation where geometry and equilibrium relaxation replace clocked transistors altogether; requires primary material, noise, drift, and speed qualification before hardware adoption |

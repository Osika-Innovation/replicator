Dissolve — material recovery

Recover useful components and feedstock through controlled, material-specific processes. Design the build so its next life is easier.

v1.1 · reviewed 2026-11-13 · Role: recovery process architecture. This revision replaces the phase-reversal account with material, energy and qualification requirements. It adds no project bench result. Topology belongs to Architecture; storage detail to The Stores; actuation and sensing to the carrier papers.

0 · The objective

The recovery objective is usable material with known composition and condition. Start with the least disruptive operation that preserves value: release a designed joint, recover a component, separate a known material stream, or reprocess material into qualified feedstock.

Assembly and recovery share a platform, models and observations. Their recipes are different. Reversing the phase of a wave does not reverse welding, cure, oxidation, diffusion or mixing. Each process specifies the change it causes and the material that leaves it.

Design for recovery during fabrication: accessible interfaces, identifiable materials, separable modules and a recorded process history make the later operation simpler. The first recovery task should use a known part and material system, with a measurable reuse objective.

1 · Assembly and removal are process programs

OperationProgramAcceptance
AssembleFeed → position → join → inspectGeometry, bond and functional requirements
RecoverIdentify → release or reprocess → collect → sort → qualifyRecovery yield, composition, contamination and reuse performance

The same actuator may serve both programs when its operating envelope fits. A recovery route can also require a replaceable process module, collection system or external finishing step. Select those elements by the material task; shared hardware is an optimization result, not a consequence of a sign change.

2 · Time reversal is a focusing tool

Time-reversal acoustics records a propagated signal and re-emits its reversed waveform to refocus energy. In a sufficiently stable reciprocal system, the measured propagation paths help construct a useful focusing drive. The aperture, bandwidth, loss and changes in the medium determine performance. See Fink, basic principles and the experimental study.

This is a candidate way to deliver energy to a known region. It does not identify which bond should break or guarantee selective disassembly. At a changing joint, update the response model from observations and qualify the process against local temperature, damage and release criteria. Repeated focusing is useful only when those observations support the intended outcome.

3 · Absorption is not unbinding

Coherent perfect absorption occurs when a suitable lossy structure and its incident coherent channels are matched so the incoming energy is absorbed. The effect has a specific frequency, input-state and material dependence. It is described by Chong et al. and demonstrated optically by Wan et al.

Absorption answers where input energy goes. Recovery also needs a mechanism that converts that energy into the desired material change. The result may be heating, deformation or damage rather than clean release. CPA neither chooses the machine's plate count nor guarantees absorption by an arbitrary object. Select aperture and actuation geometry from the task's coupling and coverage.

4 · Choose the least costly material route

RouteCandidate purposeRequired qualification
Release an interfaceRecover a reusable component or separate compatible materialsJoint release mechanism, access, residual contamination and component damage
Soften or remeltReshape a compatible material or return it to a processable formTemperature/history, degradation, composition, oxidation and solidification behavior
Mechanical or acoustic processingSeparate, clean or reduce material for another processParticle distribution, damage, wear contamination and collection yield
Chemical separationRecover selected constituents from a defined mixtureReaction/selectivity, reagents, separation yield, residuals and feedstock purity

A melt is not automatically pure stock; a softened weld need not return its original grains. Material transformations depend on composition and history. Choose the output specification first, then the process that can meet it.

Book energy for actuation, heating, phase change, chemistry, separation, pumping and cooling. Any recovered electrical or thermal energy is measured as a separate output. Unbinding does not generally release useful energy, and a recovery module earns its complexity by reducing the measured total cost.

5 · Collect, separate and account for the output

The material ledger includes the original part, added reagents and process media, reusable output, residues, captured emissions and remaining inventory. Report collection losses and measurement uncertainty. Stock does not increase by exactly the object's mass when some material is lost, rejected or combined with process inputs.

Keep material streams separated where possible. Choose storage form by the material's stability and the next manufacturing process; a universal salt-or-ash rule cannot replace compatibility and composition checks. Returned stock carries lot identity, processing history and an acceptance result.

The stores paper owns the broader inventory concept. A recovery implementation supplies a specific material route and its mass balance before joining that loop.

6 · The first recovery demonstration

  1. Select a known joint, component or single-material scrap stream.
  2. Declare the intended recovered output, reuse test and numerical acceptance criteria.
  3. Measure input material, process additions and the energy delivered.
  4. Run the release or reprocessing sequence with the required observations.
  5. Collect and characterize all significant output streams.
  6. Use the recovered material or component in a second accepted part.

This closes a useful recovery loop. Expand to more materials and mixtures by qualifying each added operation. Compare in-house recovery with outsourced recycling by total human effort, yield, energy and the quality required by the next build.

7 · Evidence and handoffs

Published focusing and absorption experiments establish their reported wave behaviors. They are inputs to process design, not measurements of this machine's recovery performance. A simulated recovery sequence records its material model and assumptions; a physical result records specimens, settings, measurement methods and uncertainty.

Hand the resulting recipe, input/output specification, material balance, energy record and reuse test to the system architecture and upgrade plan. The acoustic, optical and control modules remain shared wherever that reduces the work required to manufacture and recover the next useful component.

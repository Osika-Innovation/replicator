The Replicator

Principles — what the machine is, the small set of principles it runs on, and where each principle is worked out in full. Written to be read first, and to be explainable onward to a non-technical reader.

v1.3 · reviewed 2026-11-13 · Role: public principles and paper map. This revision clarifies the engineering programme, control limits, manufacturing programs and recovery processes. Historical RH-1 illustrations remain design references. Current implementation results belong to the twin; this overview adds no bench result.

0 · The machine we are building

The Replicator programme develops a modular manufacturing platform whose fields move material, whose process modules join it, and whose sensors verify the result. Its long-term functions are scan, assemble and recover. The first configuration establishes a useful manufacturing loop and produces parts that improve the same platform.

Acoustics supplies candidate manipulation forces; electromagnetic and optical modules add sensing or processing functions when they solve a defined task. The digital twin connects geometry, material response, control, fabrication recipes and inspection. AI uses that model to explore designs, prepare experiments and reduce human setup and rework.

Keep three records distinct: the desired part, observations of a workpiece, and the executable manufacturing program. A boundary response can be stored compactly as modes or chords; replaying that response is not a general recipe for recreating matter. A completed part must pass the dimensional, material and functional criteria its use requires.

RH-1, the free-standing three-plate column, is a specified design instance in the hardware papers. The modular prototype and its progression are described in the upgrade guide.

The simple picture. Measure the workpiece, choose the next useful action, apply it and check the result. Put repeatable functions into geometry where that simplifies the machine. Let software handle adaptation. Build one useful upgrade, install it and repeat.

2 · Principle: shape fields from accessible boundaries

Choose actuator positions, geometry and drive signals together. A known linear medium maps boundary excitation to an interior field; an inverse design finds inputs that approximate the field needed for a task. Finite apertures, bandwidth, loss, power and independent input count determine what can be reached.

A boundary representation theorem is not a guarantee of arbitrary control or complete imaging from a few ports. The same transfer model must account for unobservable regions and indistinguishable responses. Validate useful control and sensing on the required payload and workspace.

Fields can act over a volume and support parallel operations. Manufacturing rate still follows the feed, transport, capture, joining and inspection process. The machine moves grains, droplets or other process units; counting atoms and multiplying by carrier frequency does not establish a build rate.

Division of work. The earlier R1/R1-b architecture explored optical addressing with acoustic manipulation and electromagnetic heating. The modular prototype uses conventional electronic control and acquisition. Select each carrier by its measured coupling to the payload, available sensing information and total implementation effort. Shared field notation does not make their actuators or material interactions interchangeable.

Structured optical fields. Polarization, phase and spatial structure provide candidate sensing and control channels. A topological label describes a field configuration; useful robustness must be checked under the losses, scatterers and disturbances of the actual module. 4 · Principle: every surface is a computed hologram

An instance's EM-plate spiral gratings, its graded phononic hex screens, and its q-BIC leakage maps are not three tricks — they are three outputs of one design procedure. Record the interference of the reference wave the feed actually launches, Ψref, against the object wave wanted in the volume, Ψobj, and write it as surface impedance: Z(r) = X₀ + M·Re{Ψ*ref·Ψobj}. Running the reference wave across the modulated surface reconstructs the object wave. The plate pattern thus stops being a shape we choose and becomes a pattern we compute — the passive, printed half of the boundary joins the drive as compiler output; a printed hologram is fixed, so it steers by frequency, the driven half changes every microsecond, and together they are the boundary condition. MEASURED art on both carriers (leaky-wave holographic antennas; printed acoustic holograms). Full treatment: Mechanical Construction §2–§3.

Chirality (R4, adopted 2026-07-30). Driven structures are chiral and separately addressable; passive structures are achiral and neutral. Handedness mixed inseparably at fine scale cancels (the double gyroid is achiral); handedness in independently fed enantiomeric substructures — mirrored caps, contrawound winding pairs — is a knob. Diversity is only useful if addressable; unaddressable diversity averages to zero.

Historical design example: one driver plate, dimensioned (added 2026-08-03, revised same day). The principle above — hologram, not shape choice — is not just a claim about the abstract surface impedance equation; it is buildable, and working the actual numbers surfaces real corrections, including one that questioned a whole capability. Each of the three plate assemblies is a dual-carrier coaxial horn: three PZT elements drive a common throat, a contrawound torus doubles as the horn's ring-radial flare, a graded ceramic gyroid carries the flare from throat to mouth while doubling as the EM dielectric, and one perforated plate — a Fibonacci-mesh micro-horn field (21×34 crossed spirals, the Vogel/sunflower lattice) plus 12 equiangular EM slots — is the shared radiating face. The bore recorded in the build spec (Ø130) turns out to be sized for a capability the machine doesn't need at that shared axis: neither the optical stem nor fine-feedstock transport needs more than a few mm: the real driver was bulk ingot-scale induction melting, which wants its own coil, not a shared one. Dropping that capability shrinks the bore to Ø12; the throat, no longer bore-constrained, is set instead by three PZT elements not overlapping at 120° (Ø36). Refitting the horn profile to the Webster area law with the new numbers gives a radius compression of 11.2:1 — this time overshooting the 6.7:1 a golden-ratio elongation would predict, having undershot it (1.84:1) before the bore fix. Landing on opposite sides of that number across two independent revisions is the point: the fit follows the physics each time, not an aesthetic prior (R3). Full derivation, the bore-sizing argument, and the ripple into the hardware spec: Mechanical Construction §3c.

5 · Principle: three functions, explicit process recipes

The long-term machine integrates scan, assembly and recovery. Each function has its own qualified operating envelope.

Assemble

  1. Specify. Define the part's material, geometry, function and acceptance test.
  2. Feed and position. Deliver the selected material and execute a reachable manipulation sequence.
  3. Join. Apply the material-specific bonding, curing or consolidation process, with its energy and thermal budget.
  4. Inspect. Measure the completed result and correct or reject it against the declared criteria.

Scan

Apply known probe signals and record calibrated observations. Infer only the properties those observations can constrain. Keep measured signals, reconstructed estimates and unresolved interior features distinct in the stored record and view.

Recover

Identify the material system, release or reprocess it through a specified mechanism, collect the outputs and qualify them for reuse. Phase reversal can help focus waves; it does not reverse bonding or purify material. Recovery carries a mass, contamination and energy ledger.

6 · Principle: observe, act and verify

The control loop compares predicted and observed responses, updates the state estimate and chooses the next action. A recorded scattering response S(f) describes the object as seen by specified ports and frequencies. It is evidence about the object, not a unique blueprint or an actuator program.

Manufacturing programs include feed, actuation, joining and inspection. Matching an echo is useful only for the features that the measurement has been shown to distinguish. Part acceptance can therefore combine response measurements, dimensional metrology, material characterization and a functional test.

Choose update rates from the process dynamics and uncertainty budget. Resonant gain, settling, calibration lifetime and thermal drift trade against one another. The digital twin and hardware use the same job interfaces so each measurement can improve subsequent predictions.

7 · Principle: the carriers talk to each other

Sound is a density wave and density sets the refractive index, so the two carriers interact in flight — parametrically, with negligible energy exchange but large information value. Three interactions: sound steers light (the acoustic hologram as a reprogrammable volumetric optic — modulation-class in air, genuine beam shaping in the melt); light reads sound (schlieren/vibrometry — the machine photographs its own acoustic field, an independent witness for the forward model); light writes sound (photoacoustics — a virtual acoustic source at any optically visible point, including on the workpiece: position diversity with nothing moving). All three MEASURED art; machine-level use is design. Full treatment: The Two Carriers §6b — the canonical home of the crossings.

8 · Principle: reusable interfaces, explicit scaling

Keep geometry, material properties, ports and calibration in shared module records. A new body, aperture, sensor or processing head should reuse the job and inspection interfaces wherever possible. This supports one enduring platform with successive configurations.

Scaling requires new calculations for force, heat, loss, bandwidth, fabrication and metrology. Wavelength ratios are useful design variables, but no single geometric scaling preserves every process. The long-term architecture and upgrade path track those dependencies.

8b · Evidence that travels with the design

Use direct statements about what each record establishes: a published experiment, a calculation, a simulation, a project measurement or a proposed mechanism. Published results belong to their reported apparatus and conditions; project measurements identify the actual module and calibration.

Keep design choices and evidence status separate. A preferred architecture can be pursued decisively while its qualification work remains explicit. Numerical results carry their configuration, model assumptions and acceptance criteria. Reconstructed content retains its inferred status even when it looks convincing or agrees with other estimates.

This paper is the single canonical home for the Replicator's machine topology — the boundary-coverage ladder, the plate assembly and its channel topology, the stack, the windings, and the matter loop. Other papers in the network link here rather than restating this material; if another paper is found restating it, that paper gets the pointer and this one keeps the text. Conversely this paper does not own: the wave principles (Principles), carrier element physics (Phonons, Photons, Two Carriers), the control software (Field Compiler), the nuclear sector (Transmutation), or any specific build's dimensions (Hardware Spec, Mechanical Construction — RH-1 is one instance of this architecture, not the architecture).

The license for boundary-only control (the Kirchhoff–Helmholtz theorem: the field on a closed surface determines the field everywhere inside it) is owned by Principles §2; this paper takes it as given and builds the machine family it implies.

1 · The boundary-coverage ladder

1.1 Why two plates — the minimal machine

The entry rung is two opposed concentric-ring phased-array plates — a bullseye above, a bullseye below — enclosing a build volume. Both of the machine's jobs force the transporter geometry:

  1. The mold. A standing-wave field whose nodal manifold holds the object's pattern requires counter-propagating fields; opposed arrays are the canonical realization, and opposed-array levitation rigs are published, working art MEASURED.
  2. The drain. The REMOVE verb is coherent perfect absorption (Principles); CPA of a mode radiating into both half-spaces requires conjugate control of both counter-propagating channels — one plate cannot absorb what escapes toward the other side. The transporter look is the CPA boundary condition made visible MEASURED (CPA/anti-laser demonstrated in optics and acoustics).
Fig. 1 — The minimal machine (§1.1). Counter-propagating cone-basis fields write the standing-wave mold; the phase-conjugate REMOVE verb needs both escape channels — hence the transporter look; the axis bore carries feedstock and metrology; the rim harvests drained power. One plate alone is the table replicator — full ADD, partial REMOVE (§1.2).

2 · The plate assembly

2.1 Addressing

Each plate is an addressing surface for the field between them:

  • Ring n ↔ band n: rings carry transducers for frequency band f₀·2ⁿ — literal octaves. (First builds are single-band; multiband is the design intent.)
  • Azimuthal phase ↔ tone: a phase winding eiℓθ around a ring selects azimuthal mode ℓ; (band, ℓ) is the mode address.
  • Differential pairs: elements mirrored across the plate axis are driven push–pull, giving every address a signed (±) drive — the polarity axis.
  • Scaffold patterns: the icosahedral projection printed on the plates makes the twelve-port A₅ drive patterns available as a basis; the science paper's primary geometric control (Transmutation §8) compares A₅-symmetric drive to scrambled-phase drive of identical power.

2.2 The twelve-channel plate (cone basis)

The bullseye's natural mode family is conical: a ring driven at radial wavevector kr with azimuthal winding ℓ synthesizes a Bessel (axicon) beam — a cone whose angle is set by ring geometry and whose orientation by the phase winding MEASURED. Six chosen (angle, azimuth) cone-modes form a six-element basis — Russell's six-cones-from-the-cube (his BB/DD panels) as the plate's eigenbasis rather than an imposed pattern (provenance only, never load-bearing).

We standardize on twelve drive channels = six cones × two, two hardware-real readings composing: an I/Q pair gives each cone an arbitrary complex weight (twelve real channels = six complex degrees of freedom), and a differential pair gives push–pull drive natively on dual-H-bridge hardware — the ± polarity axis as circuit topology. Six complex axes are the icosahedron's twelve vertices in antipodal pairs: the plate is the projected twelve-port screen, and twelve channels is the port count the existing chamber firmware stack (per-port PIO arrays, twelve-channel drivers) already speaks. 2.3 Substrate: the electrical bus and the coil final stage — with the supersession history

The channel architecture is substrate-agnostic: every candidate transducer is electrically driven — a piezo disc is an EM→pressure converter, a laser diode an EM→optical converter, a coil is EM undisguised. The twelve-channel I/Q bus is the standard; the final stage is a per-ring configuration. What the bare EM field does without a converter, with measured anchors:

  • Conductive feedstock: EM levitation melting / cold-crucible practice — coils levitate, contain, stir and heat melts contactlessly MEASURED (industrial).
  • Charged feedstock: ion trapping is the most precise matter placement in existence (single-atom Paul traps) MEASURED. Once matter is ionized, EM coupling is total — the purist strategy is to change the matter's phase, not the field.
  • Neutral polarizable feedstock: dielectrophoresis MEASURED; diamagnetic levitation at brute-force fields MEASURED. The honest weak spot is cold neutral insulators (χ ~ 10⁻⁵) — the one regime where an acoustic final stage genuinely earns its place.
  • The nuclear sector is EM-native by necessity — every knob there is a charge-sector variable; argument and sector live in Transmutation.
  • Octave addressing is native only to EM: coils and antennas span Hz→GHz on one channel type, whereas piezo elements are narrowband mechanical resonators. The ring-n = f₀·2ⁿ scheme of §2.1 is an EM dial by construction.
  • The coil wants to be conical, on three established grounds MEASURED: (i) conical inductors have no single self-resonance (every turn a different diameter) and are the standard component of ultra-broadband bias-tees — the exact per-channel part the dual-band scheme below requires; (ii) the conical log-spiral antenna (Rumsey 1957; Dyson 1959) is frequency-independent because its geometry is defined by angles alone, and its active region moves along the cone with frequency — high octaves radiate near the tip, low near the base — so the octave ladder is laid out spatially along the axis; (iii) tapered/conical coils are the incumbent geometry of EM levitation-melting, because the cone produces the supporting field gradient.
  • The flat-plate version exists as current hardware MEASURED: EBG-backed Archimedean spiral antennas (e.g. Luadang et al., Sci. Rep. 2025) put 3.4 octaves (1.34–14 GHz, 165% bandwidth) on a printed disc — two spiral arms (the ± differential pair in copper; winding sense sets circular polarization) over concentric rings of high-impedance EBG cells (an artificial magnetic conductor: engineered zero-phase boundary that keeps a one-sided plate thin and forward-directed — directly the single-plate table rung's need). The octaves stack radially (active region at circumference ≈ λ: high bands at center, low at rim) — §2.1's ring-addressing as published GHz practice.

The two final stages coexist in one part: a voice-coil exciter is a coil in a permanent magnet's gap — in the audio band the coil's force moves the mass (sound); above the mechanical rolloff the same current radiates a near-field magnetic field, a loop inductor by construction. A summed waveform through a per-channel bias-T thus frequency-division-multiplexes both substrates on the same twelve channels: acoustic octaves on the mechanical stage, RF octaves (up to the coil's self-resonance, roughly low-MHz — induction and dielectrophoresis territory) on the electromagnetic stage — the octave ladder continuing through a single device. The permanent magnet adds a colocated static bias field B₀, which with the RF field B₁ is the magnetic-resonance addressing configuration, plus pressure on the same spot: a three-field port from a commodity part DERIVED (multiplexing physics is textbook; the three-field port as a fabrication tool is untested).

2.4 The carbon realization (graphene / CNT)

Carbon's ground-state chemistry self-assembles the screen geometry: C₆₀ is a Goldberg sphere; a nanotube's chirality indices (n,m) are the screen's GP(m,n) indices on a cylinder, with metallic-vs-semiconducting function set by n−m mod 3 (chirality determines function — the screen-parity story as materials science); and carbon nanocones take quantized cone angles set by the number of pentagonal disclinations in the tip (1–5) — Dyson cones with the port-count written into the apex MEASURED throughout. Engineering consequences:

  • The missing octaves: gated-graphene plasmonics compresses wavelengths ~100× and owns the THz band — precisely the gap between the coil band (dies at low-MHz self-resonance) and optical. The Rumsey spiral in graphene keeps its geometry and gains the middle of the ladder MEASURED (THz graphene-antenna literature).
  • Address as software: the plasmon resonance is Fermi-level (gate-voltage) tunable — the active region moves electrically, making ring/band addressing reprogrammable without changing drive frequency.
  • Cold ionization: CNT field-emission tips (enhancement ~10³) provide low-voltage feedstock ionization — the enabling part for the ionized-matter purist route of §2.3 MEASURED.
  • Feedstock plumbing: aligned sub-nm CNT membranes (industrial-scale since 2018, MatterShift / Sci. Adv.) impose single-file 1D confinement — encounter geometry forced by the pore rather than left to 3D diffusion — with anomalously fast (slip-flow) transport MEASURED. Their collar-side role belongs to Transmutation §5.1.
  • The REMOVE verb in carbon: coherent perfect absorption is demonstrated in graphene systems — a gate-tunable CPA sheet MEASURED.
  • The harvest layer (rectennas): a rectenna — antenna + tunnel diode — converts coherent field directly to DC, closing the REMOVE verb's energy loop (drained field returns to the bus instead of becoming heat). For coherent narrowband fields — precisely our drained-mode regime — rectenna conversion is measured at >80–90% at microwave (power-beaming heritage) MEASURED; CNT optical rectennas (vertically aligned forests with femtosecond MIM tunnel-diode tips, Cola group 2015) extend the principle to light but at ≪1% demonstrated efficiency — deploy-grade at microwave/THz, frontier at optical OPEN there. Doctrine note: the tunnel diode is the χ nonlinearity at the port — antenna and nonlinear element fused in one carbon structure.

Efficiency honesty: single-CNT radiators are impedance-mismatched (quantum resistance ~6.5 kΩ) and graphene plasmons are lossy at room temperature — the carbon gains are size (~100×), band access, and electrical programmability, delivered by arrays and bundles, not per-element radiation efficiency.

2.5 The dual-carrier plate assembly — front end and face (rulings R2–R4, topology summary)

Under the current ruling (§2.3, R3 amendment) each plate assembly carries both carriers. The topology, summarized here with element-level detail owned by the carrier papers (Phonons, Photons):

  • Acoustic front end (R3 — horn + gyroid, architecture B). Three piezo elements at a common throat → torus ring-radial horn (the bore-winding torus doubles as the horn flare — compact-depth because the outward sweep does the flaring) → graded gyroid volume → patterned plate. The horn section is an ellipse fit to the Webster area law — the fitted aspect ratio is reported, never decreed DERIVED. The gyroid is a ceramic (dielectric) skeleton, density graded and golden-angle perturbed, never uniform — a uniform gyroid is a periodic phononic crystal (a filter); graded is a 3D rainbow library. Terminal layer opens into the plate perforations. Compression-driver framing: piezos = diaphragms, horn = transformer, plate = a deliberately rich, known phase plug. Cell-size law, viscothermal floor, and drive detail: Phonons.
  • Plate face (R4 — Fibonacci crossed-spiral micro-horn mesh). One milled/printed conductor plate carries both carriers by scale separation: crossed golden log-spiral V-groove families from both faces with Fibonacci-different arm counts (equal counts give a degenerate periodic rhombic lattice — forbidden; Fibonacci counts make the crossing cells the Vogel lattice, the whole aperiodic dimple field from two spiral toolpaths) DERIVED; grooves deep enough to intersect, each diamond cell perforating as a biconical micro-horn; and 12 single-handed equiangular through-slots (the EM toroidal-pulse launcher) cut through the mesh. At the EM launch band the sub-λ mesh reads as solid conductor; acoustically both slots and mesh transmit — physics is the diplexer. Groove/slot element detail: Photons (EM) and Phonons (acoustic).
  • Chirality doctrine (R4, one sentence): driven structures are chiral and separately addressable; passive structures are achiral and neutral. Racemic mixing at cell scale cancels (the double gyroid is achiral — MEASURED photonics); both-handedness capability lives only in independently fed enantiomeric substructures. The 3-element ring synthesizes acoustic OAM m = ±1 by phasing alone — drive-side chirality, no geometric doubling needed.
  • The optical stem (R2/R2-b, summary). The bore optical channel is one bidirectional holographic port: it writes a programmable acoustic source aperture on any optically visible surface (patterned Hadamard-class bases, not spot-scanning), speaks the caps' toroidal-pulse alphabet at optical frequency (self-healing, isodiffracting), and reads back by state tomography. The full treatment — launcher art, tomography, topological alphabet — is owned by Photons and Field Compiler §17.7.

3 · The stack — open resonator, three plates, two chambers

The machine is a cylinder: plates facing each other is the transporter configuration, and the column is the smallest polite body around a stack of them. The stack topology below is the free-standing baseline adopted by the 2026-07-30 rulings session (REPLICATOR_CAP_STACK_RULINGS_2026-07-30.md, R8–R11), rendered here as the canonical text; provenance table in §7.

3.1 Open resonator: fields aim, walls watch (R8)

Each cap hologram carries a focusing (quadratic phase) term so the cap pair forms a stable open resonator (flat-parallel is the worst case) — plate-to-plate energy topology with exponentially small spill where the Fresnel gate N = a²/λL permits (a = aperture radius, L = plate spacing). Confinement by aiming is a high-frequency privilege; which bands clear the gate depends only on N, a size-free ratio. Instance example (RH-1: a = 140 mm, L = 300 mm — see Hardware Spec):

Walls demoted from boundary to instrument: they close EMC for the unbeamable bands, drive the muscle band, provide high-angle coverage (the specular gap needs steep rays), hold the reverberant DOF budget, and contain the acoustic field for the room's sake.

3.2 Three-plate stack, two mirrored chambers (R9)

Top, middle, and bottom plate assemblies; build chamber above, storage chamber below; the middle plate is double-faced — two cap stacks back to back, the mirrored-pair logic promoted to an interior bulkhead, radiating up into the build volume and down into storage. The storage chamber is an active instrument, not a bin: acoustophoretic sorting (the same Δκ/Δρ contrast the machine senses and grips — MEASURED art), incoming QC via the identical tomography stack, staging and pre-heat. Same-size twin = one bill of materials; later options: calibration chamber, disassembly/reclaim chamber.

3.3 Axial field-driven transport (R10)

Feedstock moves through the co-axial bores (doctrine already routes it there; preserves azimuthal symmetry; shortest path). Three contrawound winding pairs stacked on one axis = six independent circuits = traveling-wave synthesis: a linear-induction elevator for conductive feedstock plus acoustic tube conveying for the rest. The lift is a field, not a mechanism. The bore shares duty with the optical sightline by aperture partition or time-multiplex.

3.4 Presented form: free-standing; plan A is ordinary air (R11)

Ruling (operator, 2026-07-30): the machine operates in normal atmosphere. The plan-A working band is whatever plain air supports across the chamber; absorption per pass stays single-digit dB because the short closed geometry is itself the mitigation (dB per pass, not dB/m of sonar range). Medium conditioning (dry gas, pressurization, fluid coupling — absorption ∝ f², classical part ∝ 1/P; each rung MEASURED physics) is demoted to the roadmap shelf: real levers, not preconditions, and nothing in the architecture forecloses them.

The free-standing form stands on the stack itself: top = build chamber, middle = storage twin, base = feedstock reserve + power + compute. Deck line: two chambers — one holds the object, one holds the matter it will become; between them, one plate.

4 · Windings — contrawound pairs, separately fed (R5)

Every winding assembly is an enantiomeric (contrawound) torus-knot pair with two independent feed circuits (MEASURED art: contrawound toroidal helical antennas). Sum mode = pure confined poloidal flux (induction / potential port); difference mode = ring dipole; blend = continuous helicity/twist knob. One both-handed wire on one amp is racemic rank-1 — forbidden (the chirality doctrine of §2.5 applied to windings). With atom-placement manufacturing the pair renders as embedded σ(x) filaments; the two-feed requirement survives every generation — it is an information constraint, not a fabrication one. Winding-element detail (turns, feed networks, materials): Photons and, for the instance, Mechanical Construction.

The honest inversion: the vanishing verb is the easy half. Every stage of disassembly-and-return is measured industrial art; assembly carries the research program. A chemistry-only Replicator with these stores — no transmutation at all — already covers food, ceramics, and most plastics: roughly ten cheap elements. The fiction agrees on the mundane side too: shipboard replicators run on matter reclamation — the dishes are next week's tea — conservation law, not scriptwriting.

6 · Where the binding constraint lives — patterning bandwidth

The placement-rate argument is decisive. A cup is ~3×10²⁴ atoms; a five-minute build requires ~10²² placements per second. Serial architectures are dead on arrival; only whole-boundary parallel patterning survives (table). The doctrine's "the drive pattern is the tool, addressing the whole volume at once" is thereby promoted from elegant to mandatory: no serial machine can ever be a replicator.

The synthesis: energy was never the deep constraint. Both residual constraints — patterning bandwidth and the nuclear sector's dynamics — reduce to one knob: coherence/fidelity of the boundary drive pattern. The mining doctrine reached the identical conclusion from the opposite direction ("there is one knob: EML-tree fidelity"), and it is the honest reading of every fabricator account in the lore corpus: the impressive thing was never the power source — the field pattern is the factory.
